Cost Reference

Approximate cost range for one IVF cycle

Sample reference prices below (in USD). Actual costs vary significantly by clinic, plan and individual circumstances — treat your consultant's formal quote as authoritative.

ItemCost RangeNotes
Base stimulation + egg retrieval cycle$12,000 – $18,000Includes medication, monitoring, retrieval procedure
PGT genetic screening (per embryo batch)$3,000 – $6,000Billed by number of embryos tested
Frozen embryo transfer (FET)$4,000 – $7,000Includes transfer procedure and luteal support
Third-party egg donation$25,000 – $45,000Includes donor compensation and coordination fees
Gestational surrogacy (full process)$120,000 – $200,000Includes surrogate compensation, legal and medical costs
Interpreter / travel coordination$3,000 – $8,000Billed by number of service days and scope
