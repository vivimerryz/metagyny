About Us

Supporting every family through this unique journey

Metagyny was founded on a simple idea: information asymmetry shouldn't be one more burden on the road to parenthood.

Our Story

(Sample content — replace with your platform's real story.) Metagyny was co-founded by a group of families who lived through overseas IVF journeys themselves, alongside medical information professionals. We know firsthand how an unfamiliar healthcare system, language barriers, and overwhelming amounts of information can make every decision feel stressful. That's why we set out to build a neutral, transparent, user-centered platform that brings together scattered information, clinic data, and professional consulting in one place.

Our Mission

To give every person considering IVF in the United States enough real, understandable information before they decide — and professional, attentive support throughout the entire process.

How We Work

  • Content review: Articles are timestamped with their last update, and important medical content should always be weighed against a licensed physician's advice.
  • Clinic data: The clinic directory is continually maintained, helping you compare options across multiple dimensions.
  • Consulting: Consultants follow consistent training and a clear service process, with confidentiality as a baseline.
  • Privacy: We take every user's privacy seriously — information we collect is used only to provide consulting services.
