Not sure what the labels on a clinic mean?

Rating numbers (e.g. 9.4/10) come from the patient-review platform FertilityIQ and reflect real patient satisfaction scores; the "SART Verified" label means the clinic is an official member of the Society for Assisted Reproductive Technology (SART), which requires regularly submitting audited cycle and live-birth data to the CDC — an industry-recognized baseline of legitimacy. For some academic medical centers (such as Stanford, UCLA, and UCSF), we show official CDC 2022 annual cycle and live-birth data directly instead, which is more authoritative than patient reviews — these hospitals simply aren't typically active on consumer review platforms, so they have no star rating. That doesn't mean the clinic isn't good.
