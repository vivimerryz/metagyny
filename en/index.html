Why the United States

Four strengths of US IVF

General information only — success rates and eligibility depend on individual physician evaluation.

Mature Technology

Third-generation IVF (PGT) genetic screening is widely used, with highly standardized embryology labs.

Established Legal Framework

Many states have clear legal protections for third-party reproduction (egg donation, gestational surrogacy).

More Choice

Policies around embryo sex screening and multi-embryo banking are comparatively flexible, widening your options.

Mature Support Network

An established ecosystem of medical and logistics services, with Chinese-speaking teams commonly available.

The Process

A general timeline for US IVF

Actual timelines vary by individual health status and clinic scheduling — for reference only.

1 1–2 weeks

Initial Consult & Records Review

Submit your prior test results; a consultant helps match you with a physician and arranges a remote consultation.

2 2–4 weeks

Visa & Travel Planning

Assistance preparing visa documents, booking flights and lodging, and planning your travel schedule.

3 10–14 days

Stimulation & Egg Retrieval

Once in the US, complete stimulation injections, follicle monitoring and the egg retrieval procedure under physician guidance.

4 5–7 days

Lab Culture & Genetic Testing

Fertilized eggs are cultured to the blastocyst stage in the lab; PGT genetic screening can be performed if desired.

5 1 day

Embryo Transfer

The best embryo is selected and transferred into the uterine lining, followed by a luteal support plan.

6 10–14 days

Pregnancy Test & Follow-up

A blood test confirms pregnancy after transfer; follow-up continues with a local physician or platform consultant after you return home.
