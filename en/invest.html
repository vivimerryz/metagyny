Fertility care
still doesn't have its own data layer

In 2022, the US performed 435,000 IVF cycles, resulting in nearly 100,000 babies — 2.6% of all US births. Yet patients still rely on forums, word of mouth, and scattered information to make one of the highest-stakes medical decisions of their lives. We're building the first layer of data infrastructure for this industry.

What We Believe

Every IVF cycle is a high-stakes choice — starting at $20–30K, with success rates that vary by clinic, and a decision window of just a few weeks — and the information backing that decision is still scattered forum posts and one-sided sales pitches from agencies. We believe making this industry transparent, comparable, and data-backed is, on its own, a multi-billion-dollar business.

Problem & Opportunity

A high-value, high-frequency decision with zero infrastructure

The Problem

The information infrastructure for fertility care decisions lags far behind the size of the market:

  • Success-rate reporting standards differ by clinic, so patients can't directly compare
  • Pricing is opaque, and actual spend often far exceeds expectations
  • International patients traveling to the US can barely find a trustworthy Chinese-language information source
  • The decision window is short (usually just a few weeks), but the cost of gathering good information is high

The Opportunity

Three structural trends are expanding this market:

  • Delayed childbearing age continues to drive growing demand for ART cycles
  • US advantages in third-generation IVF and third-party reproduction law attract patients traveling from around the world
  • No platform yet combines clinic data, patient decision tools, and professional consulting into a single product
