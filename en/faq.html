FAQ

The answers to what you're probably wondering

Didn't find what you're looking for? Book a consult directly for a one-on-one answer.

Roughly how much should I budget in total for US IVF?

A base medical cycle (stimulation + retrieval + transfer) typically runs about $20,000–$35,000; total costs rise significantly if egg donation or surrogacy is involved. Your exact budget depends on the plan and clinic you choose — book a consult for a detailed quote.

Besides medical fees, what other costs should I prepare for?

Also budget for round-trip flights, local lodging, interpretation and escort services, visa processing, and insurance — generally 20–30% of the medical cost as a buffer for other expenses.

What kind of visa do I need for IVF treatment in the US?

Most patients travel on a B1/B2 business/tourism visa. Prepare proof of funds and a medical appointment letter in advance; check the latest requirements with a US embassy/consulate.

What if my visa application is denied?

You can review the reason for denial, strengthen your documentation, and reapply. Our consultants can help you understand common denial reasons and how to prepare stronger materials.

Up to what age is IVF still likely to succeed?

Success depends on age, ovarian reserve, and several other individual factors. We recommend starting with baseline tests like AMH and antral follicle count, then getting a physician's personalized assessment — age alone isn't the whole picture.

Is ovarian stimulation painful? Are there side effects?

Stimulation involves daily injections; some patients experience bloating or mood swings, and a small number may develop ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS). Your doctor will adjust the protocol based on how your body responds — flag any discomfort with your medical team right away.

Can I choose the sex of the embryo?

Most US states legally allow learning and selecting embryo sex during PGT genetic screening, but specific policies vary by clinic and state — check feasibility with a consultant.

Is third-party egg donation / surrogacy legal in the US?

Laws vary significantly by state — California and Nevada, for example, are relatively favorable toward third-party reproduction — but not every state permits commercial surrogacy. Research the specific laws of your target state carefully before choosing a plan.

What citizenship and legal status does a child born via third-party reproduction have?

A child born in the US typically acquires US citizenship. Specifics around identity recognition and how that connects to citizenship/registration back home can change with policy, so consult an immigration and legal professional for the latest guidance.

Does the platform charge for consultations?

The initial consultation is completely free, and a consultant will provide guidance based on your situation. If you'd like the platform to coordinate clinic arrangements and travel afterward, we'll explain any service fees clearly before you confirm — there are no hidden charges.

Still have questions?

Everyone's situation and needs are different — the most accurate answer comes from a one-on-one conversation.
